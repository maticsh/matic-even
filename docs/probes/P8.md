# P8: What fires when the phone backgrounds the Even app or locks?

## The question

When the phone itself sends the Even app to the background (you switch apps), or the phone locks, which events reach the plugin, and in what order?

## Why it matters

OS overlays on the glasses fire `FOREGROUND_ENTER_EVENT` when they open and `FOREGROUND_EXIT_EVENT` when they close ([H7](../findings/lifecycle.md#h7-os-overlays-fire-foreground-events-enter-on-open-exit-on-close)). Nobody has logged what the phone side fires. Apps need this to know when to save state and when to re-arm sockets, audio and location.

## Steps

This probe needs a beta install. A QR sideload loads from a dev server. Nobody has tested whether it backgrounds the way an installed build does.

1. In `apps/probe/`, run `npm run pack`. It writes `probe.ehpk`.
2. In the Even Hub developer portal, upload the `.ehpk` as a build and push it to a beta group that holds your own account.
3. On the phone: Even app, then Me, then Beta tester, then Install. Launch the probe from the glasses menu.
4. On the main page, wait for a few heartbeats.
5. Switch to another app on the phone for 30 s. Come back.
6. Lock the phone for 30 s. Unlock it.
7. Read the raw event list on the phone page. The fourth line on the glasses shows the last three events.

## What to record

- The raw event list with clock times, for each step.
- What you did at each clock time.
- Whether `gaps` grew.
- Phone model, OS version, Even app version, glasses firmware.

## What we know so far

- Not run yet. It needs a beta install.
- The probe logs raw `enter`, `exit` and `sysExit` events before any filter, so it is ready for this test.
