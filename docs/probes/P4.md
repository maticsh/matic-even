# P4: How fast does the wearing sensor react?

## The question

When the wearer takes the glasses off or puts them on, how long until `onDeviceStatusChanged` reports the new `isWearing` value?

## Why it matters

An app that pauses or hides content when the glasses come off needs to know the delay. A slow sensor means the app should not wait on it.

## Steps

1. Check that wear detection is on in the Even app's settings. With it off, `isWearing` never changes ([H17](../findings/sensors.md#h17-wear-detection-is-a-user-setting)).
2. Run the probe with the dev server, so the log streams to `apps/probe/logs/` ([apps/probe/README.md](../../apps/probe/README.md#run-it-on-your-glasses)).
3. Stay on the main page. It shows `wearing: yes` or `wearing: no`, and the clock time it changed.
4. Start a stopwatch at the moment you take the glasses off. Stop it when you see the change on the phone page.
5. Put the glasses back on and time it again.
6. Repeat each direction 5 times.

The CSV log has a `wearing` row for each change, with its time in ms. Note the clock time of each take-off, so you can line the rows up.

## What to record

- Each delay in seconds, off and on.
- The `wearing` rows from the CSV.
- Phone model, OS version, Even app version, glasses firmware, SDK version.

## What we know so far

- The sensor reports off and on while a plugin is open ([H6](../findings/sensors.md#h6-the-wearing-sensor-reports-while-a-plugin-is-open)).
- Nobody has timed the delay yet.
