# P13: Do image updates wipe text that was upgraded earlier?

## The question

A page has text containers and image containers. You upgrade the text with `textContainerUpgrade`, then send a few images with `updateImageRawData`. On glass, does the upgraded text stay?

## Why it matters

In simulator 0.9.5, upgraded text vanishes about a second after the next few image sends. It comes back only when it is sent again. If the glasses do the same, any app that animates an image next to text loses its text.

## Steps

The probe's `edge` blink sends 13 images while two upgraded text lines stay unchanged.

1. Run the probe ([apps/probe/README.md](../../apps/probe/README.md#run-it-on-your-glasses)).
2. On the main page, look at the header (`PROBE · up ...`) and the action bar at the bottom. The probe set both with text upgrades.
3. Tap three times, one tap at a time. Wait for each blink to finish before the next tap. The third tap runs the `edge` style.
4. During `edge`, the header reads `PROBE · BLINK edge`. The probe sends it once, then sends 12 sprite frames at 500 ms, then one more. It does not send the header or the action bar again during the blink.
5. Watch the header and the action bar for the whole 6 s. Do they stay?
6. Run the same steps in the simulator, and compare.

## What to record

- Whether the header and the action bar stayed during the `edge` blink, on glass.
- If they vanished: after how many frames, and how long after a send.
- The same for the simulator, with its version.
- Phone model, OS version, Even app version, glasses firmware, SDK version.

## What we know so far

- Seen in simulator 0.9.5 only.
- Not yet checked on glass.
- Until this is answered, an app can hold its animation still while text shows. Another safe choice is to send the text again after the images.
