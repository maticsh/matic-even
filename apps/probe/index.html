waiting for the glasses...
  1. Blink: tap the temple. Does the header catch your eye from the side?
  2. Wearing: take the glasses off for 10 s, then put them back on.
  3. Image: swipe. Note the ms and whether the image flickers.
  4. Long session: leave it running. Gaps over 15 s mean the app was suspended.
  5. Sense: swipe up. Follow the IMU script in the probe README. Long-press at the start of each step.
  6. Scripts: tap, then hold, and pick Scripts. Compare the glasses with the text on this page.
